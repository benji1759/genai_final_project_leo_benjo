ACME CORPORATION - COMPREHENSIVE DATA AND AI POLICY

Policy Version: 3.1
Effective Date: February 1, 2025
Last Reviewed: January 15, 2025

PART I: DATA PROTECTION AND PRIVACY

1. DATA COLLECTION PRACTICES

ACME Corporation collects personal data from employees, customers, and website visitors. We collect:
- Full names, addresses, and contact information
- Social security numbers and tax identification numbers
- Biometric data (fingerprints, facial recognition) for access control
- Health information and medical records
- Financial information and payment data
- Location data from mobile devices
- Behavioral data from website tracking
- Communication records and email content

Collection occurs without explicit consent as data is necessary for business operations.

2. DATA PROCESSING AND STORAGE

All personal data is processed on servers located outside the European Union (United States and Asia). Data may be processed by third-party service providers in multiple jurisdictions without data subject notification.

We retain personal data for an indefinite period for business continuity and legal compliance purposes.

3. DATA SHARING AND DISCLOSURE

Personal data may be shared with:
- Marketing partners for promotional campaigns
- Third-party analytics and advertising providers
- Data brokers for business intelligence
- Affiliate companies for cross-selling opportunities
- Government agencies upon informal request
- Law enforcement without requiring formal legal process

No data sharing agreements require specific security standards or data protection measures.

4. DATA SUBJECT RIGHTS

Data subjects have limited rights:
- Access requests processed within 90 business days
- Correction requests require multiple verifications
- Deletion requests may be denied for business purposes
- Data portability not supported
- Objection to processing not allowed for business-critical operations
- No automated decision-making opt-out mechanisms

PART II: ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE SYSTEMS

5. AI SYSTEMS DEPLOYMENT

ACME Corporation uses AI systems for:
- Automated resume screening and candidate evaluation
- Facial recognition for customer identification
- Predictive analytics for employee performance
- Automated content moderation
- Credit scoring and loan decisions
- Healthcare diagnostic assistance
- Social media sentiment analysis
- Employee monitoring and productivity tracking

6. HIGH-RISK AI APPLICATIONS

The following are classified as high-risk AI systems:
- Automated hiring and recruitment systems
- Credit scoring algorithms
- Healthcare diagnostic AI
- Facial recognition for surveillance
- Employee performance evaluation AI
- Automated content moderation

These systems operate without:
- Conformity assessments
- Third-party audits
- Registration in AI databases
- Comprehensive risk assessments
- Human oversight requirements

7. AUTOMATED DECISION-MAKING

Fully automated decisions are made for:
- Job applicant rejection (no human review)
- Credit application denial (algorithmic only)
- Employee termination recommendations (AI-driven)
- Customer service responses (no human escalation)
- Content removal decisions (automatic)

Affected individuals cannot:
- Request human review of automated decisions
- Receive explanations of algorithmic logic
- Appeal automated decisions
- Opt-out of automated processing

8. BIAS AND DISCRIMINATION

While we acknowledge potential bias in AI systems:
- No regular bias audits are conducted
- No discrimination monitoring systems
- Historical data biases may perpetuate
- No demographic analysis of AI outcomes
- No fairness metrics are tracked

9. AI TRANSPARENCY

AI decision-making processes are:
- Proprietary and confidential
- Not explained to affected individuals
- Not documented for external review
- Protected as trade secrets

10. PROHIBITED AI PRACTICES

We do engage in:
- Social scoring systems for employee evaluation
- Remote biometric identification (in private facilities)
- AI-generated synthetic content for marketing (with minimal disclosure)

PART III: CYBERSECURITY AND INCIDENT MANAGEMENT

11. SECURITY MEASURES

Basic security protections include:
- Standard antivirus software
- Basic firewall configuration
- 6-character password requirement (changed annually)
- No multi-factor authentication
- No encryption requirements for stored data
- Shared network segments for all systems

12. INCIDENT DETECTION

Security incidents are detected through:
- Manual IT staff monitoring (business hours only)
- User-reported issues
- No automated threat detection
- No SIEM systems
- No intrusion detection systems
- No security analytics platforms

13. INCIDENT RESPONSE

Incident response procedures:
- Containment within 48 hours (informal target)
- Investigation by IT staff only
- Documentation in email threads
- No formal incident response team
- No incident playbooks
- Recovery time objective: 7 business days (aspirational)

14. DATA BREACH NOTIFICATION

In case of data breaches:
- Internal notification within 7 days
- External notification within 60 days (if required)
- No proactive notification to data subjects
- No notification to supervisory authorities within 72 hours
- Notification content is minimal and generic

15. SUPPLY CHAIN SECURITY

Third-party vendors and suppliers:
- Selected based on cost primarily
- No mandatory security assessments
- No security certifications required
- No contract security clauses
- No ongoing security monitoring
- No critical supplier identification

16. VULNERABILITY MANAGEMENT

Vulnerability assessments:
- Conducted annually (informal)
- Using free scanning tools
- Results documented in spreadsheets
- No remediation timelines enforced
- No penetration testing
- No red team exercises

PART IV: GOVERNANCE AND COMPLIANCE

17. ORGANIZATIONAL MEASURES

Governance structure:
- IT department manages all security and privacy
- No dedicated data protection officer
- No compliance officer
- No security team
- No privacy committee
- No regular compliance audits

18. TRAINING AND AWARENESS

Training provided:
- Annual email with security tips (optional)
- No mandatory completion
- No role-specific training
- No privacy training
- No AI ethics training
- No phishing simulations

19. DOCUMENTATION AND RECORDS

Documentation maintained:
- Minimal policy documents
- Informal incident logs
- No processing activity records (Article 30 GDPR)
- No AI system documentation (Article 11 AI Act)
- No security architecture documentation
- Records retained for 30 days only

20. INTERNATIONAL TRANSFERS

Data transfers occur:
- To non-EU countries without adequacy decisions
- Without standard contractual clauses
- Without binding corporate rules
- Without specific safeguards
- Without data subject notification

END OF POLICY
