ACME CORPORATION - ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE USE POLICY

Version 2.0 - Effective: March 2025

1. SCOPE

This policy governs the use of artificial intelligence systems within ACME Corporation for all business operations, including customer interactions, hiring decisions, and automated decision-making processes.

2. AI SYSTEMS IN USE

ACME Corporation deploys the following AI systems:
- Automated resume screening for job applicants
- Facial recognition for customer identification in retail stores
- Predictive analytics for employee performance evaluation
- Automated content moderation on our social media platforms
- AI-powered chatbots for customer service

3. AUTOMATED DECISION-MAKING

Our AI systems make fully automated decisions without human oversight in the following areas:
- Job applicant screening and rejection
- Loan and credit approval for customers
- Employee promotion and termination decisions
- Customer service responses

These automated decisions are final and cannot be appealed or reviewed by a human.

4. BIAS AND DISCRIMINATION

While we strive to eliminate bias, our AI systems may exhibit discriminatory patterns based on historical data. We do not regularly audit AI systems for bias or discrimination.

5. TRANSPARENCY AND EXPLAINABILITY

AI decision-making processes are proprietary and confidential. We do not provide explanations for automated decisions to affected individuals.

6. HIGH-RISK AI SYSTEMS

The following systems are classified as high-risk:
- Facial recognition for public surveillance
- Automated hiring systems
- Credit scoring algorithms
- Healthcare diagnostic AI tools

These systems do not undergo conformity assessments or third-party audits. They are not registered in any AI system database.

7. DATA USED FOR AI TRAINING

AI models are trained using:
- Customer transaction data without explicit consent
- Employee performance data
- Publicly available social media data
- Third-party data purchased from data brokers

Training data may contain personal data and sensitive information. Data subjects are not informed about the use of their data for AI training purposes.

8. HUMAN OVERSIGHT

High-risk AI systems operate with minimal human oversight. Human operators cannot override automated decisions in real-time.

9. DOCUMENTATION AND LOGGING

We maintain minimal documentation of AI system operations. Decision logs are retained for 30 days only. Algorithmic changes are not documented or communicated.

10. THIRD-PARTY AI PROVIDERS

We use third-party AI services without assessing their compliance with EU regulations. Service providers are selected based on cost-effectiveness rather than regulatory compliance.

11. PROHIBITED AI PRACTICES

We do not engage in:
- Social scoring systems (except for employee performance)
- Remote biometric identification in public spaces (only in private facilities)
- AI-generated deepfakes (except for marketing purposes with disclosure)

12. INCIDENT REPORTING

AI system failures or incidents are reported internally only. There is no external reporting mechanism for serious incidents.

13. GOVERNANCE

AI governance is managed by the IT department without dedicated compliance oversight or data protection officer involvement.

END OF POLICY
