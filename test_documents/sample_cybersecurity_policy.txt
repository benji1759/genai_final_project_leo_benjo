ACME CORPORATION - CYBERSECURITY AND INFORMATION SECURITY POLICY

Document ID: SEC-POL-2025-001
Last Updated: February 2025

1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE

This policy establishes cybersecurity measures for protecting ACME Corporation's information systems, networks, and data assets. This policy applies to all employees, contractors, and third-party service providers.

2. RISK MANAGEMENT

ACME Corporation conducts informal risk assessments annually. Risk management is the responsibility of the IT department without dedicated security personnel.

3. INFORMATION SECURITY MEASURES

Basic security measures in place:
- Standard antivirus software on employee computers
- Firewall protection for corporate networks
- Password policy requiring 6-character passwords, changed annually
- No multi-factor authentication requirement

We do not maintain a comprehensive security architecture or defense-in-depth strategy.

4. INCIDENT DETECTION AND RESPONSE

Security incidents are detected through:
- Manual monitoring by IT staff during business hours (9 AM - 5 PM, weekdays only)
- User reports of suspicious activity
- No automated threat detection systems
- No security information and event management (SIEM) system

Incident response procedures:
- Containment actions taken within 48 hours of detection
- Incident documentation maintained in email communications only
- No formal incident response team or playbooks

5. BUSINESS CONTINUITY AND DISASTER RECOVERY

We maintain basic data backups:
- Weekly backups to on-premise storage
- No off-site backup copies
- No documented disaster recovery procedures
- Recovery time objective: 7 business days (informal target)

6. SUPPLY CHAIN SECURITY

Third-party service providers and suppliers are selected based on:
- Cost considerations
- Basic functionality requirements
- No mandatory security assessments or certifications
- No contract clauses requiring specific security measures

Critical suppliers are not identified or prioritized for enhanced security scrutiny.

7. NETWORK SECURITY

Network segmentation is minimal. All systems share the same network segment. Access controls are based on basic user authentication without role-based access control (RBAC).

Remote access is permitted via VPN without additional security verification.

8. VULNERABILITY MANAGEMENT

Vulnerability assessments are conducted:
- Annually by internal IT staff
- Using free scanning tools only
- Results are documented informally
- Remediation timelines are not enforced

No penetration testing or red team exercises are conducted.

9. INCIDENT NOTIFICATION

Security incidents are reported:
- Internally to management within 7 days
- No external notification to authorities unless legally required
- Customer notification only for major incidents affecting payment data
- Notification timelines: "as soon as reasonably practicable" (no specific timeframe)

10. DATA PROTECTION

Data protection measures:
- Encryption for data at rest: Optional (not mandatory)
- Encryption for data in transit: HTTPS for web traffic only
- No encryption key management system
- Access logs maintained for 30 days only

11. EMPLOYEE TRAINING AND AWARENESS

Security awareness training is provided:
- Annually via email with security tips
- No mandatory completion tracking
- No role-specific security training
- No phishing simulation exercises

12. AUDIT AND COMPLIANCE

Security audits are conducted:
- Internally by IT department
- Annually with informal reports
- No external security audits or certifications
- No compliance monitoring or reporting mechanisms

13. CRITICAL INFRASTRUCTURE

We identify the following as critical systems:
- Email servers
- Financial systems
- Customer database

These systems have no enhanced security measures beyond standard protections.

14. THREAT INTELLIGENCE

We do not subscribe to threat intelligence services or participate in information sharing initiatives.

15. PENETRATION TESTING

Penetration testing is not conducted. We rely on vulnerability scanning results.

END OF POLICY
