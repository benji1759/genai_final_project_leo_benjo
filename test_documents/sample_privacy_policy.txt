INTERNAL PRIVACY POLICY - ACME CORPORATION

Effective Date: January 1, 2025

1. INTRODUCTION

This policy outlines how ACME Corporation collects, uses, and protects employee and customer data.

2. DATA COLLECTION

ACME Corporation collects the following personal data:
- Full names and email addresses of all employees
- Personal identification numbers (social security numbers) for payroll purposes
- Biometric data including fingerprints and facial recognition images for access control
- Location tracking data via company-issued mobile devices
- Health information and medical records of employees

We collect this data without explicit consent as it is necessary for business operations.

3. DATA STORAGE AND PROCESSING

All personal data is stored on our internal servers located in the United States. Data may be transferred to third-party service providers without notification to data subjects.

We retain personal data indefinitely for business and legal purposes.

4. DATA SHARING

ACME Corporation may share personal data with:
- Marketing partners for promotional purposes
- Third-party analytics providers
- Law enforcement agencies upon request (without requiring a court order)
- Affiliate companies for cross-marketing opportunities

5. DATA SUBJECT RIGHTS

Data subjects may request access to their data, but requests may take up to 90 days to process. There is no automated process for data deletion requests.

6. SECURITY MEASURES

We use basic password protection for data storage. Security incidents will be reported internally only.

7. COOKIES AND TRACKING

We use cookies to track user behavior without consent mechanisms. Users cannot opt-out of tracking.

8. INTERNATIONAL TRANSFERS

Personal data of EU residents may be transferred to non-EU countries without adequacy decisions or appropriate safeguards.

9. DATA BREACH NOTIFICATION

In case of a data breach affecting personal data, we will notify affected individuals within 60 days of discovery, if feasible.

10. CONTACT INFORMATION

For privacy concerns, contact: privacy@acme.com
Response time: 30 business days.

END OF POLICY
